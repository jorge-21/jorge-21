## Featured projects

### [AI Operations Triage & Routing](https://github.com/jorge-21/ai-ops-triage)

An end-to-end NLP system for classifying unstructured banking requests while routing uncertain or sensitive cases to human review.

- Compares TF-IDF and sentence-embedding baselines across 77 banking intents
- Includes data-contract tests, calibration, error analysis, and operational-risk framing
- Serves predictions through FastAPI with a Streamlit demonstration

**Tools:** Python, scikit-learn, sentence transformers, FastAPI, Streamlit, pytest

### [Rural Credit Policy Analytics](https://github.com/jorge-21/finagro)

An applied data and policy analysis of how Finagro lending may affect Colombia's benchmark lending rate and regulatory interest-rate ceiling.

- Builds reproducible ingestion and consolidation pipelines for large public datasets
- Replicates and validates benchmark-rate calculations
- Applies shift-share decomposition and time-series models with macroeconomic controls
- Separates accounting evidence, statistical association, and causal interpretation

**Tools:** Python, pandas, Polars, statsmodels, Plotly, Altair, public-data APIs

### [Evidence-Grounded Financial Operations Copilot](https://github.com/jorge-21/evidence-grounded-financial-copilot)

A local RAG prototype for answering financial-operations questions from an approved document corpus.

- Preserves source traceability from ingestion through final answers
- Requires citations and abstains when evidence is insufficient or conflicting
- Evaluates retrieval quality, citation coverage, and answer-status decisions
- Includes a FastAPI backend and Streamlit interface

**Tools:** Python, retrieval pipelines, FastAPI, Streamlit, structured evaluation
